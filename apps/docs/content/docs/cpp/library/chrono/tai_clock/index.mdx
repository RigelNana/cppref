{/* source:cpp-chrono-tai-clock:0001 */}
The clock `std::chrono::tai_clock` is a [Clock](/docs/cpp/library/named_req/Clock) that represents [International Atomic Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Atomic_Time) (TAI). It measures time since 00:00:00, 1 January 1958, and is offset 10 seconds ahead of UTC at that date (i.e., its epoch, 1958-01-01 00:00:00 TAI, is 1957-12-31 23:59:50 UTC).

{/* source:cpp-chrono-tai-clock:0002 */}
Leap seconds are not inserted into TAI. Thus, every time a leap second is inserted into UTC, UTC falls another second behind TAI. As of December 2017, UTC is 37 seconds behind TAI, reflecting the 10-second initial offset and the 27 leap seconds inserted between 1958 and 2017. Thus, 2018-01-01 00:00:00 UTC is equivalent to 2018-01-01 00:00:37 TAI.

{/* source:cpp-chrono-tai-clock:0003 */}
`tai_clock` meets the [Clock](/docs/cpp/library/named_req/Clock) requirements. It does not meet the [TrivialClock](/docs/cpp/library/named_req/TrivialClock) requirements unless the implementation can guarantee that [`now()`](/docs/cpp/library/chrono/tai_clock/now) does not throw an exception.
