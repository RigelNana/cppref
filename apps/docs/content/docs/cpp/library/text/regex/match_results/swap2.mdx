Specializes the [`std::swap`](/docs/cpp/library/algorithm/swap) algorithm for [`std::match_results`](/docs/cpp/library/text/regex/match_results). Exchanges the contents of `x1` with those of `x2`. Effectively calls `x1.swap(x2)`.

### Parameters

{/* source:cpp-regex-match-results-swap2:0002 */}
<ParameterList>
  <Parameter name="x1, x2">
    the match_results objects whose contents will be swapped
  </Parameter>
</ParameterList>

Type requirements

- `BidirIt` must meet the requirements of [`LegacyBidirectionalIterator`](/docs/cpp/library/named_req/BidirectionalIterator).
- `Alloc` must meet the requirements of [`Allocator`](/docs/cpp/library/named_req/Allocator).
