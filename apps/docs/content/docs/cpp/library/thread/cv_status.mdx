{/* source:cpp-thread-cv-status:0001 */}
The scoped enumeration `std::cv_status` describes whether a timed wait returned because of timeout or not.

{/* source:cpp-thread-cv-status:0002 */}
`std::cv_status` is used by the `wait_for` and `wait_until` member functions of [`std::condition_variable`](/docs/cpp/library/thread/condition_variable) and [`std::condition_variable_any`](/docs/cpp/library/thread/condition_variable_any).

### Member constants

{/* source:cpp-thread-cv-status:0003 */}
<DescriptionList>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`no_timeout`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>the condition variable was awakened with `notify_all`, `notify_one`, or spuriously</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`timeout`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>the condition variable was awakened by timeout expiration</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
</DescriptionList>
