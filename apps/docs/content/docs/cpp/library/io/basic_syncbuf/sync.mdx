{/* source:cpp-io-basic-syncbuf-sync:0001 */}
First, records that a flush is pending, by updating the appropriate private flag.

{/* source:cpp-io-basic-syncbuf-sync:0002 */}
Then, if the current emit-on-sync policy is `true`, makes a call to [`emit()`](/docs/cpp/library/io/basic_syncbuf/emit).

{/* source:cpp-io-basic-syncbuf-sync:0003 */}
Otherwise (if the emit-on-sync policy is `false`, which is the default), the flush is suspended until [`emit()`](/docs/cpp/library/io/basic_syncbuf/emit) is called, such as through [`std::basic_osyncstream::emit()`](/docs/cpp/library/io/basic_osyncstream/emit) or [`std::basic_osyncstream::~basic_osyncstream`](/docs/cpp/library/io/basic_osyncstream/~basic_osyncstream)

### Parameters

{/* source:cpp-io-basic-syncbuf-sync:0004 */}
(none)

### Notes

{/* source:cpp-io-basic-syncbuf-sync:0005 */}
`sync()` or its equivalent is implicitly called by `close()`, `seekoff()`, and `seekpos()` and explicitly called by [`std::basic_streambuf::pubsync()`](/docs/cpp/library/io/basic_streambuf/pubsync)
