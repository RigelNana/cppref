    2) The move-constructor that initializes the underlying [`coroutine_`](/docs/cpp/library/ranges/generator#Data_members) with `std::exchange(other.coroutine_, {})`, and the underlying stack of coroutine handles ([`active_`](/docs/cpp/library/ranges/generator#Data_members)) with `std::exchange(other.active_, nullptr)`. Note, that the iterators, previously obtained from `other`, are not invalidated, but become iterators into `*this`.
