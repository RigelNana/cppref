The scoped enumeration type `pointer_safety` lists the pointer safety modes supported by C++.

### Enumeration constants

{/* source:cpp-memory-gc-pointer-safety:0002 */}
<DescriptionList>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`pointer_safety::strict`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>Only safely-derived pointers (pointers to objects allocated with new or subobjects thereof) may be dereferenced or deallocated. Garbage collector may be active.</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`pointer_safety::preferred`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>All pointers are considered valid and may be dereferenced or deallocated. A reachability-based leak detector may be active.</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`pointer_safety::relaxed`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>All pointers are considered valid and may be dereferenced or deallocated.</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
</DescriptionList>
