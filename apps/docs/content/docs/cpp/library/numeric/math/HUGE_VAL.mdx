{/* source:cpp-numeric-math-huge-val:0001 */}
The macros `HUGE_VALF`, `HUGE_VAL` and `HUGE_VALL` macros expand to positive floating point constant expressions which compare equal to the values returned by floating-point functions and operators in case of overflow (see [math_errhandling](/docs/cpp/library/numeric/math/math_errhandling)).

{/* source:cpp-numeric-math-huge-val:0002 */}
Constant
 Explanation

<DescriptionList>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`HUGE_VALF`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>Expands to positive `float` expression that indicates overflow</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`HUGE_VAL`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>Expands to positive `double` expression that indicates overflow, not necessarily representable as a `float`</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`HUGE_VALL`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>Expands to positive `long double` expression that indicates overflow, not necessarily representable as a `float` or `double`</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
</DescriptionList>

{/* source:cpp-numeric-math-huge-val:0003 */}
On implementations that support floating-point infinities, these macros always expand to the positive infinities of `float`, `double`, and `long double`, respectively.

### Notes

{/* source:cpp-numeric-math-huge-val:0004 */}
On implementations that do not support floating-point infinities, these macros expand to the maximum finite number of their respective types.

{/* source:cpp-numeric-math-huge-val:0005 */}
C++98 added `float` and `long double` overloads of mathematical functions. There is a problem that the `float` overloads cannot return `HUGE_VAL` to indicate overflow because this macro is not guaranteed to be representable as a `float`.

{/* source:cpp-numeric-math-huge-val:0006 */}
[LWG issue 357](https://cplusplus.github.io/LWG/issue357) was raised to target this problem. LWG found that C99 has the same problem (`float` and `long double` overloads were also added in C99), and C99 introduced new macros `HUGE_VALF` and `HUGE_VALL` to solve the problem. Therefore the issue was closed and the C99 resolution was adopted in C++11.
