{/* source:cpp-atomic-atomic-flag-notify-one:0001 */}
Performs atomic notifying operations.

{/* source:cpp-atomic-atomic-flag-notify-one:0002 */}
If there is a thread blocked in atomic waiting operation (i.e. [`wait()`](/docs/cpp/library/atomic/atomic_flag/wait)) on `*this`, then unblocks at least one such thread; otherwise does nothing.

### Notes

{/* source:cpp-atomic-atomic-flag-notify-one:0003 */}
This form of change-detection is often more efficient than simple polling or pure spinlocks.
