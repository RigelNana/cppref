This header was originally in the C standard library as [`<float.h>`](/docs/c/library/types/limits#Limits_of_floating-point_types).

{/* source:cpp-header-cfloat:0001 */}

This header is part of the [type support](/docs/cpp/library/utility/types) library, in particular, it is a part of the [C numeric limits interface](/docs/cpp/library/utility/types/climits).

## Macros

<DescriptionList>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`FLT_RADIX`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>the radix (integer base) used by the representation of all three floating-point types (macro constant)</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`DECIMAL_DIG` <InlineRevision since="C++11" /></DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>conversion from `long double` to decimal with at least `DECIMAL_DIG` digits and back to `long double` is the identity conversion: this is the decimal precision required to serialize/deserialize a `long double` (see also [`std::numeric_limits::max_digits10`](/docs/cpp/library/utility/types/numeric_limits/max_digits10)) (macro constant)</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`FLT_DECIMAL_DIG`, `DBL_DECIMAL_DIG`, `LDBL_DECIMAL_DIG` <InlineRevision since="C++17" /></DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>conversion from `float`/`double`/`long double` to decimal with at least `FLT_DECIMAL_DIG`/`DBL_DECIMAL_DIG`/`LDBL_DECIMAL_DIG` digits and back is the identity conversion: this is the decimal precision required to serialize/deserialize a floating-point value (see also [`std::numeric_limits::max_digits10`](/docs/cpp/library/utility/types/numeric_limits/max_digits10)). Defined to at least `6`, `10`, and `10` respectively, or `9` for IEEE float and `17` for IEEE double. (macro constant)</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`FLT_MIN`, `DBL_MIN`, `LDBL_MIN`</DescriptionTerm>

  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`FLT_TRUE_MIN`, `DBL_TRUE_MIN`, `LDBL_TRUE_MIN` <InlineRevision since="C++17" /></DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>minimum positive value of `float`, `double` and `long double` respectively (macro constant)</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`FLT_MAX`, `DBL_MAX`, `LDBL_MAX`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>maximum finite value of `float`, `double` and `long double` respectively (macro constant)</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`FLT_EPSILON`, `DBL_EPSILON`, `LDBL_EPSILON`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>difference between `1.0` and the next representable value for `float`, `double` and `long double` respectively (macro constant)</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`FLT_DIG`, `DBL_DIG`, `LDBL_DIG`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>number of decimal digits that are guaranteed to be preserved in text → `float`/`double`/`long double` → text roundtrip without change due to rounding or overflow (see [`std::numeric_limits::digits10`](/docs/cpp/library/utility/types/numeric_limits/digits10) for explanation) (macro constant)</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`FLT_MANT_DIG`, `DBL_MANT_DIG`, `LDBL_MANT_DIG`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>number of base `FLT_RADIX` digits that can be represented without losing precision for `float`, `double` and `long double` respectively (macro constant)</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`FLT_MIN_EXP`, `DBL_MIN_EXP`, `LDBL_MIN_EXP`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>minimum negative integer such that `FLT_RADIX` raised by power one less than that integer is a normalized `float`, `double` and `long double` respectively (macro constant)</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`FLT_MIN_10_EXP`, `DBL_MIN_10_EXP`, `LDBL_MIN_10_EXP`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>minimum negative integer such that `10` raised to that power is a normalized `float`, `double` and `long double` respectively (macro constant)</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`FLT_MAX_EXP`, `DBL_MAX_EXP`, `LDBL_MAX_EXP`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>maximum positive integer such that `FLT_RADIX` raised by power one less than that integer is a representable finite `float`, `double` and `long double` respectively (macro constant)</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`FLT_MAX_10_EXP`, `DBL_MAX_10_EXP`, `LDBL_MAX_10_EXP`</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>maximum positive integer such that `10` raised to that power is a representable finite `float`, `double` and `long double` respectively (macro constant)</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>[`FLT_ROUNDS`](/docs/cpp/library/utility/types/climits/FLT_ROUNDS)</DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>default rounding mode of floating-point arithmetic (macro constant)</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>[`FLT_EVAL_METHOD`](/docs/cpp/library/utility/types/climits/FLT_EVAL_METHOD) <InlineRevision since="C++11" /></DescriptionTerm>
    <DescriptionBody>specifies in what precision all arithmetic operations are done (macro constant)</DescriptionBody>
  </DescriptionItem>
  <DescriptionItem>
    <DescriptionTerm>`FLT_HAS_SUBNORM`, `DBL_HAS_SUBNORM`, `LDBL_HAS_SUBNORM` <InlineRevision since="C++17" /></DescriptionTerm>

  </DescriptionItem>
</DescriptionList>
